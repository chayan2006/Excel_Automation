---
title: Accounts and signing in
url: /login
---
You must sign in before you can use any part of the website: the control room, uploads, downloads, the review workspace and the website assistant.

- There is no public sign-up. Accounts are created by the website's administrator; to get an account, ask your administrator.
- Sign in with your username and password on the sign-in page. Usernames are not case-sensitive.
- If you forget your password, ask your administrator to set a new one. Setting a new password signs the account out everywhere it was signed in.
- A sign-in lasts until 12 hours pass without activity. After that you are asked to sign in again, and then you are returned to the page you were on.
- To sign out, select "Sign out": at the top right of the Excel control room, or at the bottom of the left sidebar in the review workspace.
- Your account name is recorded in the review workspace's activity log and on the documents you upload, change, confirm, export, reject or delete.
- All accounts work in the same workspace: everyone sees the same master workbook, processing queue and documents.
