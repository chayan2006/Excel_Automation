This website is the Excel Control Room. It offers three services:

- Workbook automation: monthly Excel workbooks are checked against the processing rules and merged into one master workbook, Master.xlsx. Changes can be previewed before they are applied, and the master is backed up automatically before every change.
- Telecom invoice extraction: invoice PDFs from Airtel, Jio, Vodafone Idea or another provider are read, and their key values are saved to an Excel report.
- Invoice review workspace: values extracted from invoice PDFs are checked by a person against the PDF, confirmed, and exported to Excel.

Every part of the website requires signing in with an account.

Main pages:
- Excel control room (/): the master workbook, the processing queue, invoice extraction, run history and backups.
- Review workspace (/review/documents): the document list and the review screen for invoices.

The website assistant (the "Ask a question" button) answers questions from these help pages only. It cannot see your workbooks, invoices, documents or run results.
